## ⚠️ Not Recommended (But Viable)

### Web Scraping & Data Services
**Pros:** Good market size ($718M-1.1B), proven demand  
**Cons:** HIGH legal risk (GDPR, ToS violations), constant maintenance, platform dependency  
**Verdict:** Only with legal counsel, niche specialization

### API Monitoring & Alert Services  
**Pros:** Stable market, recurring revenue  
**Cons:** Mature/competitive market, slower growth, infrastructure costs  
**Verdict:** Viable but not optimal for autonomous agents

### Affiliate Marketing Automation
**Pros:** Low startup cost, high margins  
**Cons:** VERY long time to profitability (18-24+ months), extremely competitive, requires audience building  
**Verdict:** Better as supplementary income, not primary
