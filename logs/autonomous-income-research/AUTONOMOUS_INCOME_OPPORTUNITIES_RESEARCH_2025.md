### Legal and Compliance Considerations

**Critical Legal Issues:**

1. **Copyright Law:**
   - Cannot republish copyrighted content without permission
   - Fair use exceptions for research/analysis
   - Risk: $150,000 per work statutory damages

2. **Terms of Service (ToS):**
   - Violating ToS can lead to cease & desist, lawsuits
   - Browsewrap (passive) less enforceable than Clickwrap (active acceptance)
   - Landmark case: HiQ Labs v. LinkedIn (public data scraping allowed)

3. **GDPR (EU) Compliance:**
   - Personal data requires lawful basis (consent, legitimate interest)
   - Data minimization mandatory
   - Transparency and data retention limits
   - Penalties: €20M or 4% global revenue (whichever higher)
   - Must respect "right to be forgotten"

4. **CCPA (California) Compliance:**
   - Consumer rights over personal information
   - Must provide opt-out mechanisms
   - Less prescriptive than GDPR but still significant

5. **CFAA (US Computer Fraud and Abuse Act):**
   - Prohibits "unauthorized access" to computer systems
   - HiQ v. LinkedIn ruling: Publicly accessible data scraping is legal
   - Avoid password-protected areas, respect robots.txt

6. **robots.txt Protocol:**
   - Not legally binding but shows good faith
   - Ignoring can weaken legal position
   - Industry best practice: respect robots.txt

**Compliance Best Practices:**
- Only scrape publicly accessible data
- Respect rate limits (don't DDoS servers)
- Honor robots.txt files
- Avoid collecting PII without consent
- Implement data retention policies
- Use transparent User-Agent strings
- Provide clear privacy policy
- Consider legal review of ToS before scraping

**Liability Risk Level: HIGH**
- Regular legal consultation recommended: $500-$1,500/month
- Terms of service for your own business: $2,000-$5,000 one-time
- Ongoing compliance audits: $3,000-$10,000 annually
