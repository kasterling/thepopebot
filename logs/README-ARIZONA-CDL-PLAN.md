## 🎯 Plan Summary - Key Facts

### **The Opportunity**

**What:** Relocate to Prescott Valley, AZ and complete Roehl Transport's paid CDL training program in Phoenix

**Why:** 
- No tuition costs (you're paid to train)
- Guaranteed job from day one
- First year income: $50,000-$70,000+
- Career path with growth potential
- Great benefits from day one

**Timeline:** 12 weeks from start to solo driving

**Total Investment Required:** $7,000-$8,000 (moving costs + emergency fund)

### **The Numbers**

| Metric | Amount | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Moving Costs** | $4,100-$6,900 | One-time |
| **Monthly Expenses** | $2,700 avg | Prescott Valley |
| **Training Length** | 3 weeks class + 3 weeks OTR | Total 6-9 weeks to solo |
| **First Year Income** | $54,000-$65,000 | Conservative estimate |
| **Break-Even Point** | Month 5 | Cumulative positive cash flow |
| **Year-End Savings** | $19,000-$31,000 | After all expenses |

### **The Location**

**Prescott Valley, Arizona**
- 90 miles north of Phoenix (~1.5 hour drive)
- Population: ~45,500
- Elevation: 5,100 ft (cooler than Phoenix)
- Average rent: $1,250-$1,900/month (studio to 1-bedroom)
- Safe, growing community
- Four-season climate (mild)

**Phoenix Training Facility**
- Roehl Transport Phoenix Terminal
- 4909 W Lower Buckeye Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85043
- Phone: 715-591-7050

### **The Training Program**

**Phase 1: Classroom & Yard (3 Weeks)**
- Location: Phoenix terminal
- Housing and meals provided by Roehl
- Learn basic vehicle operations
- Obtain Commercial Learner's Permit (CLP)
- Take CDL skills test at end of week 3
- Paid at end of week 3

**Phase 2: Over-the-Road Training (3+ Weeks)**
- One-on-one with certified trainer
- NOT team driving - trainer coaches from passenger seat
- Live freight, real routes
- Sleep in truck (separate bunks)
- Paid daily rate (2-week delay)

**Phase 3: Solo Driving (Ongoing)**
- Your own truck assignment
- Daily support from fleet training manager
- Flatbed National fleet: 11-14 days out, 3 days home
- Paid by mileage (weekly, 1-week delayed)

### **The Timeline**

```
Weeks 1-4:   Pre-move preparation
Week 5:      Move to Prescott Valley
Weeks 6-8:   Phase 1 training (classroom)
Weeks 9-11:  Phase 2 training (OTR)
Week 12+:    Phase 3 solo driving
Month 5:     Break-even / positive cash flow
Month 12:    One year milestone
```

### **The Financial Reality**

**First 4 Months:** Cash flow is NEGATIVE
- This is expected and normal
- Emergency fund covers this period
- Pay is delayed during training
- Must have $7,000-$8,000 saved before moving

**Months 5-12:** Cash flow is POSITIVE
- Income increases steadily
- Expenses remain stable
- Rapid savings accumulation
- Financial stress decreases

**By End of Year 1:**
- $19,000-$31,000 in net savings
- $55,000-$65,000 earned
- 12 months CDL experience
- Positioned for higher pay

## ✅ Critical Success Factors

### **YOU MUST HAVE:**

1. ✅ **Minimum $7,000-$8,000 liquid savings**
   - Covers moving costs
   - Covers 4 months negative cash flow
   - Emergency cushion
   - Non-negotiable requirement

2. ✅ **Clean driving record**
   - No major violations (DUI, reckless driving, etc.)
   - No excessive speeding tickets
   - Valid driver license with 1+ year experience
   - Background check must pass

3. ✅ **Pass DOT medical exam**
   - Vision: 20/40 or better (with correction OK)
   - Hearing: Adequate
   - Blood pressure: Under 140/90 preferred
   - No disqualifying medical conditions

4. ✅ **Commitment to complete training**
   - 6-9 weeks of intensive training
   - First 3 weeks away from home (lodging provided)
   - Next 3 weeks on the road with trainer
   - Can't quit halfway through

5. ✅ **Realistic expectations**
   - First 4 months are financially tough
   - Training is hard work (physically and mentally)
   - Long hours and time away from home
   - Not a get-rich-quick scheme

### **YOU SHOULD HAVE:**

- Good credit score (600+) for apartment rental
- Reliable transportation during Phase 1
- Basic physical fitness (climbing in/out of truck, coupling trailers)
- Strong work ethic and determination
- Support system (family/friends who understand commitment)

### **IT HELPS TO HAVE:**

- Previous manual transmission driving experience
- Mechanical aptitude
- Good spatial awareness (backing, parking)
- Stress management skills
- Flexibility and adaptability

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Underestimating moving costs** → Budget $7,000+ minimum
2. **Skipping emergency fund** → You NEED 4 months expenses saved
3. **Choosing expensive apartment** → Keep rent under $1,600/month
4. **Not studying before training** → Start CDL manual NOW
5. **Ignoring medical issues** → Get DOT physical early to identify problems
6. **Poor credit preparation** → Check and fix credit before apartment applications
7. **No transportation plan** → Need reliable vehicle for Phase 1 commute
8. **Quitting too early** → First months are hardest, stick it out
9. **Unrealistic income expectations** → Income starts low, builds over time
10. **Not reading Roehl contract** → Understand your commitments fully
