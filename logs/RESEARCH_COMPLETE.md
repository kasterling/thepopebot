**Price:** May 2026 corn at $4.42, testing critical $4.40 resistance  
**Trend:** Mixed to cautiously bullish  
**Key Driver:** Record exports vs. large supplies

### Critical Insights Discovered

1. **Record Export Demand:** USDA raised 2025-26 exports by 100 million bushels to all-time high of 3.3 billion bushels - significantly supportive

2. **Brazil Weather Risk:** Safrinha corn planting window narrowing with heavy rains forecast for late February, potentially delaying planting and threatening late-season crop development

3. **Supply Overhang:** Despite export strength, ending stocks at 2.127 billion bushels (potentially heading to 7-year high) continues capping rallies

4. **Ethanol Weakness:** Corn for ethanol running behind USDA projections despite production recovery; efficiency gains reducing corn usage per gallon

5. **Policy Catalyst:** Year-round E15 push could add 2+ billion bushels of demand if Congress acts

6. **Technical Setup:** Breakout above $4.40 targets $4.65+; failure returns to $4.30 support
