STRATEGY: VALUE AREA + ATR (A-TIER + B-TIER) 

1. Identify the Value Area (Market Profile): 
   - Look for the fattest node in the Market Profile—the Point of Control (PoC). This is where 70% of trading happened. 
   - If price is above the PoC and shows strong buying pressure (from Footprint Charts), it could signal a potential reversal or continuation. 

2. Use ATR for Risk Management: 
   - Calculate the ATR to determine volatility. 
   - Set your stop loss at least 1.5x ATR away from your entry to avoid being stopped out by normal volatility. 
   - Adjust position size based on ATR: smaller positions during high volatility (large ATR), larger positions during low volatility (small ATR). 

3. Entry Rules: 
   - Enter a long trade if price is above the PoC and shows strong buying pressure (from Footprint Charts). 
   - Enter a short trade if price is below the PoC and shows strong selling pressure. 

4. Exit Rules: 
   - Take profit at the opposite side of the Value Area (e.g., if you entered long above the PoC, target the lower end of the Value Area). 
   - Use ATR to set trailing stops for dynamic exits. 

This strategy combines the power of Market Profile (A-tier) and ATR (B-tier) to create a robust, risk-managed approach.
