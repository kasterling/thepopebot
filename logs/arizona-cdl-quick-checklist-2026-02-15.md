## 📋 Arizona Driver License Requirements

**Bring to MVD:**
1. Current out-of-state driver license
2. Proof of authorized presence (birth certificate OR U.S. passport)
3. Social Security number (card not required, just the number)
4. Two Arizona address proofs (utility bill, bank statement, lease, insurance)

**For Real ID (optional but recommended):**
- All of the above PLUS
- Two documents showing current Arizona address
- If name changed: marriage cert or divorce decree

**Cost:** $25 for standard license

**Notes:**
- No appointment needed
- No written or road test if you have valid out-of-state license
- May need vision exam
- Process takes about 1-2 hours

## 🚛 Roehl Training Quick Facts

**Phase 1: Classroom (3 Weeks)**
- Location: Phoenix terminal
- Housing: Provided by Roehl
- Meals: Most provided
- Study: General knowledge, air brakes, combination vehicles
- Test: Get CLP, then CDL at end of Phase 1
- Pay: Paid at end of week 3

**Phase 2: OTR Training (3+ Weeks)**
- One-on-one with trainer
- NOT team driving - trainer coaches while you drive
- Live freight, real routes
- Sleep in truck (separate bunks)
- Pay: Daily rate, paid 2 weeks after start

**Phase 3: Solo Driving (Ongoing)**
- Get your own truck
- Daily support from fleet training manager
- Schedule: 11-14 days out, 3 days home (Flatbed National)
- Pay: Mileage rate, paid weekly (1 week delayed)

**Total Training Time:** 6-9 weeks before solo

## 📝 DOT Medical Exam Checklist

**What to Bring:**
- [ ] Photo ID
- [ ] Social Security card
- [ ] Eyeglasses/contacts (if needed)
- [ ] Hearing aids (if applicable)
- [ ] List of current medications
- [ ] Medical history documentation
- [ ] $75-$150 cash/card for payment

**Requirements to Pass:**
- Vision: 20/40 or better each eye (with correction OK)
- Hearing: Forced whisper at 5 feet
- Blood pressure: Under 140/90 (preferred)
- No disqualifying conditions

**Tips:**
- Get good sleep night before
- Avoid caffeine day of exam (BP test)
- Bring medical records if you have conditions
- Be honest - lying can disqualify you permanently

**Find Examiner:**
- nationalregistry.fmcsa.dot.gov
- Search "Prescott Valley, AZ" or "Phoenix, AZ"
- Verify FMCSA certification

**Must Study Topics:**
- [ ] General Knowledge (all drivers)
- [ ] Air Brakes (all drivers)
- [ ] Combination Vehicles (Class A)
- [ ] Pre-Trip Inspection (memorize)
- [ ] Basic Control Skills
- [ ] Road Test Procedures

**Study Resources:**
- Arizona CDL Manual: apps.azdot.gov/files/mvd/mvd-forms-lib/40-7802.pdf
- Practice Tests: driving-tests.org/arizona/az-cdl-practice-test
- Roehl Practice Tests: roehl.jobs/cdl-practice-tests
- High Road Training: truckingtruth.com/cdl-training-program

**Test Requirements:**
- 80% passing score on each knowledge test
- CLP must be held 14 days before CDL skills test
- Can re-test next business day if you fail

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Underestimating Moving Costs** → Save $7,000-$8,000 minimum
2. **Skipping Emergency Fund** → First 4 months have negative cash flow
3. **Not Studying for CDL** → Start studying BEFORE training begins
4. **Medical Issues** → Get DOT physical ASAP to identify any problems
5. **Expensive Apartment** → Keep rent under $1,600 first year
6. **Not Reading Roehl Contract** → Understand your commitments
7. **Quitting Too Early** → First few months are hardest, stick it out
8. **Poor Credit** → Clean up credit before applying for apartment
9. **No Transportation Plan** → Need reliable vehicle for Phase 1
10. **Unrealistic Expectations** → Training is hard work, income starts slow
