# Sterling Intelligence Briefing — GBP/JPY Strategy

## Overview

This briefing focuses on GBP/JPY (Sterling/Yen) forex pair analysis using a 4-hour (H4) timeframe perspective.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. **Market Data Collection**
- Current GBP/JPY exchange rate
- Recent price action (last 24-48 hours)
- Key economic news from UK and Japan
- Central bank policy updates (BoE, BoJ)

### 2. **Sentiment Analysis**
- **Bullish indicators**: Sterling strength, risk-on sentiment, positive UK economic data
- **Bearish indicators**: Yen safe-haven demand, UK economic weakness, risk-off sentiment
- **Neutral factors**: Mixed signals, consolidation patterns

### 3. **H4 Technical Outlook**
- **Trend**: Current direction on 4-hour chart (uptrend/downtrend/ranging)
- **Support/Resistance**: Key levels to watch
- **Momentum**: Are we seeing acceleration or deceleration?
- **Patterns**: Any notable chart formations

### 4. **Key Drivers**
- UK economic data (GDP, inflation, employment)
- BoE monetary policy stance
- Japan economic indicators
- BoJ policy (especially intervention potential)
- Global risk sentiment
- Cross-asset correlations (equities, bonds, commodities)

## Report Structure

1. **Current Rate & 24h Change**
2. **Market Sentiment** (Bullish/Bearish/Neutral with reasoning)
3. **Key News Headlines** (UK & Japan)
4. **H4 Technical Outlook** (Trend, levels, momentum)
5. **What to Watch** (Upcoming events/catalysts)

## Tone
- Professional, concise, actionable
- Focus on facts and observable market behavior
- No predictions — describe current conditions and key levels
- Always include disclaimer: *"This briefing is for informational purposes only and does not constitute trading advice."*
