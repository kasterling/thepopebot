# Sterling Intelligence Strategy

## Overview
This document defines the analytical framework for GBP/JPY (Sterling/Yen) H4 (4-hour) timeframe analysis and briefings.

## Market Analysis Framework

### 1. Rate & Trend Assessment
- **Current Rate**: Latest GBP/JPY exchange rate
- **Trend Direction**: Bullish/Bearish/Ranging based on price action
- **Momentum**: RSI and momentum indicators
- **Recent Performance**: Daily/weekly percentage changes

### 2. Key Technical Levels (H4 Timeframe)

#### Support Levels
- **Primary Support**: Major support zone where price is likely to find buyers
- **Secondary Support**: Additional support levels below current price
- **Critical Support**: Key level that would invalidate bullish thesis if broken

#### Resistance Levels
- **Primary Resistance**: Immediate overhead resistance
- **Secondary Resistance**: Next key resistance above
- **Major Resistance**: Significant multi-week/month resistance

### 3. Fundamental Drivers

#### Sterling (GBP) Factors
- Bank of England (BoE) monetary policy
- UK economic data (GDP, inflation, employment)
- Political developments
- Market risk sentiment

#### Yen (JPY) Factors
- Bank of Japan (BoJ) monetary policy stance
- Japanese economic indicators
- Safe-haven flows
- Global risk appetite

### 4. Sentiment Analysis
- **Market Sentiment**: Overall bullish/bearish/neutral positioning
- **Catalyst Watch**: Upcoming events that could drive volatility
- **Risk Factors**: Key downside/upside risks

### 5. H4 Trading Outlook

#### Bullish Scenario
- Entry zones for long positions
- Target levels
- Stop-loss placement
- Probability assessment

#### Bearish Scenario
- Entry zones for short positions
- Target levels
- Stop-loss placement
- Probability assessment

#### Range-Bound Scenario
- Range boundaries
- Mean reversion opportunities
- Breakout watch levels

## Briefing Format

### Sterling Intelligence Briefing Structure
1. **Executive Summary** (2-3 sentences)
2. **Current Market State** (rate, trend, momentum)
3. **Technical Outlook** (key levels, H4 bias)
4. **Fundamental Context** (major drivers)
5. **Risk Assessment** (key threats to outlook)
6. **Actionable Insight** (recommended stance)

## Update Frequency
- Primary briefings generated on scheduled cron intervals
- Ad-hoc briefings on significant market moves or events

- Real-time exchange rate feeds
- Technical analysis platforms
- Central bank communications
