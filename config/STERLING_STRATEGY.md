# Sterling Intelligence Briefing Strategy

## Overview
The Sterling Intelligence Briefing provides a focused H4 (4-hour) outlook on GBP/JPY for intraday and swing trading decisions.

## Briefing Structure

### 1. Market Snapshot
- Current GBP/JPY rate
- 24-hour change (absolute and percentage)
- Key recent price action

### 2. Fundamental Drivers
- Bank of Japan (BoJ) policy outlook and statements
- Bank of England (BoE) policy outlook and statements
- UK economic data releases (GDP, inflation, employment)
- Japan economic data (inflation, trade balance)
- Geopolitical factors affecting JPY safe-haven flows
- Japanese government intervention risk

### 3. Technical Analysis (H4 Focus)
- Current trend direction (bullish/bearish/neutral)
- Key support levels
- Key resistance levels
- Moving average alignment (20, 50, 200 period)
- Momentum indicators (RSI, MACD)
- Chart patterns

### 4. Sentiment Analysis
- Market positioning (COT data if available)
- News sentiment (bullish/bearish/mixed)
- Risk-on vs risk-off environment

### 5. H4 Outlook & Key Levels
- Directional bias for next 24-48 hours
- Entry zones (if applicable)
- Stop loss considerations
- Target zones
- Key events to watch

### 6. Risk Factors
- Central bank intervention risk
- Geopolitical events
- Economic data surprises
- Volatility considerations

## Data Sources
- Use Brave Search for:
  - Current exchange rates
  - Latest forex news and analysis
  - Central bank statements
  - Economic calendar events
  - Technical analysis from major forex sites

## Delivery
- Post comprehensive briefing to Telegram
- Use clear formatting with sections
- Include actionable insights
- Highlight key risks
