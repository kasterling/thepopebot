# Sterling (GBP/JPY) Trading Strategy

## Overview
This strategy focuses on GBP/JPY H4 timeframe analysis, tracking the Sterling Intelligence Briefing for informed trading decisions.

## Key Levels to Watch

### Resistance Levels
- **212.00** - Immediate resistance (psychological level)
- **214.44** - February 9, 2026 high
- **215.00** - February 4, 2026 peak
- **215.88** - July 2008 peak (major historical resistance)

### Support Levels
- **211.11** - 50-day SMA (key dynamic support)
- **209.68** - February 16 high turned support
- **208.14** - February 23 daily low
- **207.62** - 100-day SMA + support trendline confluence (critical floor)

## Technical Indicators

### Moving Averages
- **20-day EMA** - Short-term trend filter
- **50-day SMA** - Medium-term trend indicator (currently at 211.11)
- **100-day SMA** - Long-term support (around 207.62)

### Momentum Indicators
- **RSI** - Watch for crosses above/below 50-neutral level
  - Above 50 = Bullish momentum
  - Below 50 = Bearish pressure

## H4 Timeframe Strategy

### Bullish Scenario
- **Entry**: Confirmed break and hold above 212.00
- **Targets**: 214.44 → 215.00 → 215.88
- **Stop Loss**: Below 211.11 (50-day SMA)
- **Confirmation**: RSI above 50, price above 20-day EMA

### Bearish Scenario
- **Entry**: Break below 211.11 with confirmation
- **Targets**: 209.68 → 208.14 → 207.62
- **Stop Loss**: Above 212.10
- **Confirmation**: RSI below 50, price below 20-day EMA

### Range Trading
- **Range**: 207.62 - 212.10
- **Strategy**: Buy support, sell resistance
- **Caution**: Low conviction trades in range; prefer breakout confirmation

## Fundamental Factors to Monitor

### Bank of Japan (BOJ)
- **Dovish appointments** = Yen weakness = GBP/JPY bullish
- **Hawkish policy shift** = Yen strength = GBP/JPY bearish
- Watch for BOJ board member commentary and rate decision meetings

### Bank of England (BOE)
- **Rate hike expectations** = Sterling strength
- **Data-dependent policy** = Watch UK inflation, employment, GDP
- Monitor BOE minutes for forward guidance

### Risk Sentiment
- **Risk-on** = Yen weakness (safe-haven unwinding)
- **Risk-off** = Yen strength (safe-haven flows)
- Correlate with global equity markets

### Key Economic Data
- UK: CPI, Employment, GDP, Manufacturing PMI
- Japan: CPI, GDP, Trade Balance, Tankan Survey
- US: Fed policy (affects global risk sentiment)

## Long-Term Outlook
- **Primary Trend**: Bullish (uptrend from 123.94 in 2020)
- **Long-term Target**: 251.09 (2007 high)
- **Critical Support**: 184.02 (55-month EMA)

## Risk Management
- **Position Size**: Max 2% account risk per trade
- **Risk/Reward**: Minimum 1:2 ratio
- **Correlation**: Monitor GBP/USD and USD/JPY correlations
- **Volatility**: Adjust position size during high-impact news events

## Notes
- GBP/JPY is highly volatile - use appropriate position sizing
- Major moves often occur during London/Tokyo overlap (7:00-9:00 GMT)
- Watch for BOJ intervention if JPY weakens too rapidly
- Sterling tends to correlate with EU risk sentiment despite Brexit
