# Sterling Intelligence Briefing - GBP/JPY Trading Strategy

## Overview
This document defines the strategy for analyzing and briefing GBP/JPY (Sterling/Yen) trading opportunities.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Rate Monitoring
- Current GBP/JPY exchange rate
- Daily high/low ranges
- Week-to-date performance
- Key psychological levels (210.00, 212.00, 215.00)

### 2. Fundamental Factors
- **Bank of England (BoE)**: Rate decisions, inflation targets, Governor Bailey statements
- **Bank of Japan (BoJ)**: Rate policy, intervention threats, Governor Ueda commentary
- **UK Economic Data**: GDP, unemployment, CPI, PMI
- **Japan Economic Data**: CPI, trade balance, industrial production
- **Risk Sentiment**: Equity markets, safe-haven flows

### 3. Technical Analysis (H4 Focus)
- **Trend Direction**: Identify uptrend, downtrend, or consolidation
- **Support Levels**: Key areas where buying pressure emerges
- **Resistance Levels**: Key areas where selling pressure emerges
- **Momentum Indicators**: RSI, MACD for overbought/oversold conditions
- **Moving Averages**: 20, 50, 100, 200-period SMAs for trend confirmation
- **Chart Patterns**: Flags, triangles, double tops/bottoms, channels

### 4. Sentiment Analysis
- **Bullish Signals**: Risk-on environment, BoE hawkish, BoJ dovish, GBP strength
- **Bearish Signals**: Risk-off environment, BoE dovish, BoJ hawkish, JPY safe-haven demand
- **Neutral Signals**: Consolidation, mixed data, awaiting catalysts

### 5. H4 (4-Hour) Timeframe Focus
The 4-hour chart is the primary timeframe for intraday swing trading:
- Captures intraday trends without daily noise
- Identifies key reversal points
- Supports 1-5 day position holding periods
- Balances technical precision with fundamental narrative

## Briefing Format

### Sterling Intelligence Briefing Structure:
1. **📊 Current Rate & Movement** (last 24h change)
2. **📰 Key Headlines** (top 2-3 news items affecting GBP/JPY)
3. **📈 H4 Technical Outlook**
   - Trend direction
   - Key support levels
   - Key resistance levels
   - Momentum assessment (RSI, MACD)
4. **🔍 Sentiment Analysis** (Bullish/Bearish/Neutral with reasoning)
5. **🎯 Trading Bias** (Short-term directional bias with targets)
6. **⚠️ Risk Factors** (Events or levels that could change the outlook)

## Trade Management Principles
- **Risk Management**: Never risk more than 1-2% per trade
- **Stop Loss**: Always use stops below/above key technical levels
- **Take Profit**: Scale out at key resistance/support zones
- **Position Sizing**: Adjust based on volatility (ATR)
- **Time Horizon**: H4 timeframe = 1-5 day holding period

## Data Sources
- FXStreet, MarketPulse, ForexFactory for rates and analysis
- Central bank websites for official policy statements
- Economic calendars for data releases
- Institutional research reports (ING, Bloomberg, Reuters)
