# Sterling Intelligence - GBP/JPY Trading Strategy

## Overview
This strategy focuses on GBP/JPY (Sterling/Yen) trading on the H4 (4-hour) timeframe, analyzing technical levels, market sentiment, and fundamental drivers to generate actionable intelligence briefings.

## Key Analysis Components

### 1. Technical Levels (H4 Chart Focus)
- **Support Zones**: Key price levels where buying pressure historically emerges
- **Resistance Zones**: Key price levels where selling pressure historically emerges
- **Moving Averages**: 20 EMA, 50 SMA, 200 SMA for trend identification
- **Momentum Indicators**: RSI for overbought/oversold conditions

### 2. Market Structure
- **Trend Direction**: Identify if in uptrend, downtrend, or consolidation
- **Higher Timeframe Bias**: Align H4 analysis with daily/weekly trend
- **Key Reversals**: Watch for breaks of structure or failed tests

### 3. Fundamental Drivers
- **Bank of England Policy**: Interest rate decisions, inflation data, GDP growth
- **Bank of Japan Policy**: Monetary policy stance, intervention risks
- **Risk Sentiment**: GBP/JPY is sensitive to global risk appetite (risk-on = GBP strength, risk-off = JPY strength)
- **Economic Data**: Employment, retail sales, manufacturing PMI from both economies

### 4. Sentiment Analysis
- **Market Positioning**: Are traders net long or short?
- **Volatility**: Recent price action stability or choppy conditions
- **Correlation**: Watch GBP/USD and USD/JPY for confirmation signals

## H4 Outlook Framework

When generating the briefing, address:

1. **Current Price & Recent Action**: Where is price now? What happened in last 24-48 hours?
2. **Key Levels**: Immediate support and resistance on H4 chart
3. **Bias**: Bullish, bearish, or neutral? What would confirm each scenario?
4. **Catalysts**: Upcoming news events or technical triggers to watch
5. **Risk Factors**: What could invalidate the current outlook?

## Trading Zones (Dynamic - Update Based on Current Data)

### Bullish Scenario Triggers
- Break and hold above key resistance with momentum
- Bounce from support with bullish divergence on H4 RSI
- Risk-on sentiment with positive UK economic data

### Bearish Scenario Triggers
- Break and hold below key support with momentum
- Rejection at resistance with bearish divergence
- Risk-off sentiment or BoJ intervention concerns

### Neutral Zone
- Consolidation between defined support and resistance
- Mixed fundamental signals
- Low volatility/range-bound conditions

## Report Frequency
- Daily briefings ahead of London session open
- Special updates on major central bank announcements
- Weekly summary of net position changes
