# Sterling Intelligence Briefing Strategy

## Overview
The Sterling Intelligence Briefing provides H4 (4-hour) timeframe analysis for GBP/JPY, combining fundamental sentiment analysis with technical outlook to identify trading opportunities.

## Asset Focus
**GBP/JPY** - Sterling vs Japanese Yen cross pair

## Timeframe
**H4 (4-Hour)** - Medium-term swing trading perspective

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Rate Assessment
- Current GBP/JPY exchange rate
- Recent price action (last 24-48 hours)
- Key price levels (support/resistance)
- Daily/Weekly range context

### 2. Fundamental Sentiment Analysis
Evaluate news and economic factors affecting:

#### Sterling (GBP) Drivers:
- Bank of England policy signals
- UK economic data (GDP, inflation, employment)
- UK political developments
- Brexit-related news
- UK fiscal policy

#### Japanese Yen (JPY) Drivers:
- Bank of Japan policy stance
- Japanese economic indicators
- Risk sentiment (Yen as safe haven)
- Global risk appetite
- Japanese government intervention

#### Cross-Pair Dynamics:
- Risk-on vs risk-off environment
- Interest rate differentials
- Global equity market performance
- Commodity prices impact

### 3. Sentiment Classification
Classify overall sentiment as:
- **Bullish GBP/JPY**: Sterling strength or Yen weakness expected
- **Bearish GBP/JPY**: Sterling weakness or Yen strength expected  
- **Neutral/Consolidation**: Mixed signals or range-bound conditions

### 4. H4 Technical Outlook

#### Key Elements:
- **Trend Direction**: Uptrend, downtrend, or sideways
- **Momentum**: Strong, moderate, or weak
- **Key Levels**: 
  - Immediate support/resistance
  - Major swing highs/lows
  - Psychological levels (round numbers)
- **Chart Patterns**: If present (flags, triangles, head & shoulders, etc.)
- **Price Action Signals**: Breakouts, rejections, consolidations

#### Outlook Format:
Provide a concise 2-4 sentence summary covering:
1. Current trend and momentum
2. Key levels to watch
3. Potential scenarios (bullish/bearish/neutral)
4. Risk factors or catalysts

## Data Sources
- Real-time forex rates
- Financial news (Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, etc.)
- Central bank announcements
- Economic calendars
- Market sentiment indicators

## Update Frequency
Generated on-demand or via scheduled cron jobs for regular market updates.
