# Sterling Intelligence Strategy - GBP/JPY

## Overview
This strategy focuses on GBP/JPY (Sterling/Yen) H4 timeframe analysis combining technical levels, sentiment, and fundamental drivers.

## Key Timeframe
- **Primary**: H4 (4-hour charts)
- **Confirmation**: Daily trend alignment

## Technical Analysis Framework

### Key Levels to Monitor
- Major support/resistance zones
- Round numbers (190.00, 195.00, 200.00, etc.)
- Daily/Weekly highs and lows
- Moving averages (20 EMA, 50 EMA, 200 EMA on H4)

### Trend Identification
- **Uptrend**: Higher highs, higher lows, price above 20 EMA
- **Downtrend**: Lower highs, lower lows, price below 20 EMA
- **Ranging**: Price oscillating between clear support/resistance

### H4 Momentum Indicators
- RSI levels (oversold <30, overbought >70)
- MACD crossovers and divergences
- Volume/momentum confirmation

## Fundamental Drivers

### GBP Factors
- Bank of England policy and rate decisions
- UK economic data (GDP, inflation, employment)
- Political developments (government stability, trade relations)
- Brexit-related news

### JPY Factors
- Bank of Japan policy and intervention
- Risk sentiment (JPY as safe-haven)
- Japanese economic data
- Global risk appetite

### Cross-Currency Dynamics
- USD strength (affects both currencies)
- European economic conditions
- Global market sentiment (risk-on vs risk-off)

## Sentiment Analysis

### Bullish Signals (GBP strength)
- BoE hawkish stance / rate hike expectations
- Positive UK economic surprises
- Risk-on market environment
- JPY weakness from BoJ dovish policy

### Bearish Signals (GBP weakness)
- BoE dovish pivot / rate cut expectations
- Negative UK economic data
- Risk-off environment (JPY safe-haven bid)
- Political instability in UK

## H4 Outlook Structure

### Format for Briefing
1. **Current Price & Range**: Today's price action and key levels
2. **Trend Status**: Direction and strength on H4
3. **Key Levels**: Immediate support/resistance
4. **Sentiment**: Market positioning and news impact
5. **Outlook**: Directional bias and scenarios
6. **Risk Factors**: Events or levels that could change the view

## Risk Management
- Watch for high-impact news releases (BoE, BoJ, GDP, inflation)
- Be aware of intervention risk (especially JPY)
- Consider volatility around London and Asian session overlaps
