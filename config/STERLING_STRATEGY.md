# Sterling Intelligence Strategy

## GBP/JPY H4 Outlook Framework

### Key Data Points to Monitor

1. **Exchange Rate**: Current GBP/JPY spot rate
2. **Central Bank Policy**: 
   - Bank of England rate decisions and forward guidance
   - Bank of Japan monetary policy stance
3. **Economic Indicators**: 
   - UK consumer confidence, GDP, inflation
   - Japan inflation, wage growth, economic indicators
4. **Technical Levels**: Support/resistance on H4 (4-hour) timeframe
5. **Sentiment**: Market positioning and risk appetite

### Analysis Framework

**Bullish Factors (GBP strength / JPY weakness)**:
- BoE hawkish stance or rate hike expectations
- BoJ maintaining ultra-dovish policy
- Strong UK economic data
- Risk-on market sentiment (JPY typically weakens)
- Technical: Price above key moving averages, uptrend intact

**Bearish Factors (GBP weakness / JPY strength)**:
- BoE dovish pivot or rate cut expectations
- BoJ hawkish shift or rate hike signals
- Weak UK economic data
- Risk-off market sentiment (JPY safe-haven bid)
- Technical: Price below key moving averages, downtrend

### H4 Timeframe Considerations

- Focus on 4-hour candle patterns and momentum
- Key moving averages: 20 EMA, 50 EMA, 200 EMA
- Monitor for swing highs/lows
- Volume and momentum indicators
- Previous session range breakouts/breakdowns

### Report Structure

1. **Current Rate & Recent Movement**: Spot rate + 7-day performance
2. **Central Bank Analysis**: BoE and BoJ policy outlook
3. **News & Sentiment**: Key headlines and market mood
4. **H4 Technical Outlook**: Bias (bullish/bearish/neutral) based on above factors
5. **Key Levels**: Support and resistance zones to watch
