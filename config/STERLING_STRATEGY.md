# Sterling Strategy: Strong Weak Analysis
## Alexander Xander's Relative Strength Approach

**Source:** ForexAnalytix - FACE Interview
**Primary Focus:** GBP/JPY, but applicable to all major pairs
**Timeframes:** 4-Hour (entry), Daily (trend confirmation)
**Core Concept:** Trade strong currencies against weak currencies

---

## Core Philosophy

We trade the "Sterling" way — with discipline, patience, and rigorous risk management. The strategy rejects complexity in favor of clean, high-probability setups based on currency strength divergence.

**Sterling Principles:**
- Professional, calm, decisive
- Risk management is the keys to the kingdom
- Simplicity beats complexity
- The trend is your friend, but only with strength divergence

## Strategy Rules

### Phase 1: Currency Strength Analysis (Daily Task)

**Objective:** Identify the strongest and weakest currencies with clear divergence.

**Requirements:**
- Minimum 4-5 point separation between strong and weak
- Strong currencies: Ranking above 7/10 or top 2-3 positions
- Weak currencies: Ranking below 3/10 or bottom 2-3 positions

**Currencies to Monitor:**
- Major 8: USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, AUD, NZD, CAD
- Exotics: MXN (when available)

**Candidate Pair Creation:**
- Match strongest with weakest
- Calculate divergence spread: Strong score - Weak score
- Minimum spread: 4 points

### Phase 2: Daily Chart Confirmation

**Trend Identification:**

For LONG setups:
- Price above both 20 EMA and 50 EMA
- 20 EMA above 50 EMA
- Higher highs and higher lows pattern

For SHORT setups:
- Price below both 20 EMA and 50 EMA
- 20 EMA below 50 EMA
- Lower highs and lower lows pattern

**Key Level Marking:**
- Previous swing highs (resistance)
- Previous swing lows (support)
- Round numbers (psychological levels)

**Primary Pairs (Check in order):**
1. GBP/JPY — Sterling's flagship
2. EUR/GBP — GBP strength/weakness confirmation
3. GBP/USD — Major Sterling pair
4. Other high-divergence candidates

### Phase 3: 4-Hour Entry Execution

**Entry Type A: Breakout from Range**
1. Identify consolidation on daily chart (10-20 bars sideways)
2. Switch to 4H chart
3. Wait for 4H candle close above resistance (long) or below support (short)
4. Enter on next 4H candle open
5. Stop loss: 1 ATR below breakout level (long) or above (short)

**Entry Type B: Retracement to Key Level**
1. Confirm daily trend direction
2. Wait for price to retrace to key support (long) or resistance (short)
3. On 4H, watch for rejection candle (long wick, strong close)
4. Enter on break above rejection candle high (long)
5. Stop loss below rejection candle low

**Entry Type C: Trend Continuation**
1. Identify strong trending pair
2. Wait for pullback to 20 EMA on 4H
3. Watch for bounce off EMA
4. Enter when 4H candle closes back in trend direction
5. Stop loss below 50 EMA or recent swing low

### Phase 4: Risk Management (Critical)

**Position Sizing Formula:**
```
Position Size = (Account Size × Risk %) / (Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value)
```

**Maximum Risk:**
- 1-2% of account per trade
- Daily max loss: 3% of account
- Weekly max loss: 6% of account

**Take Profit Strategy:**
- Target 1 (50% position): 2:1 Risk/Reward ratio
- Target 2 (30% position): 4:1 Risk/Reward ratio
- Target 3 (20% position): Trail with 4H 20 EMA

**Capital Preservation Rules:**
- After 3 consecutive losses: Reduce position size by 50%
- After 5 consecutive losses: Stop trading, review journal
- Only return to full size after 2 winning trades

**Stop Loss Rules:**
- Always use hard stop loss (no mental stops)
- Never move stop closer to entry (only breakeven or trailing)

## Tactical Guidelines

### For GBP/JPY Specifically:

1. **Timeframe**: Primary analysis on H4, confirmation on H1
2. **Structure**: Only trade in direction of H4 trend
3. **Levels**: Identify Weekly/Daily support and resistance
4. **Sentiment**:
   - Bullish GBP if UK inflation rising or BoE hawkish
   - Bearish JPY if BoJ dovish or risk-on sentiment
5. **Risk**: Stop Loss 1.5x ATR away, target 2:1 minimum

### Market Hours Consideration:

- **London Open (8:00 AM GMT)**: High GBP volatility
- **Tokyo Open**: JPY pair movement
- **New York-London Overlap**: Highest liquidity
- **Avoid entries 1 hour before major news** (NFP, Central Bank decisions)

## Daily Routine

### Morning Analysis (9:00 AM)
1. Open currency strength meter
2. Record top 3 strong, bottom 3 weak currencies
3. Calculate divergence spreads
4. Create 2-3 candidate pairs with largest divergence

### Midday Check (1:00 PM)
1. Review price alerts
2. Check if entry patterns formed
3. Execute trades if criteria met

### Evening Review (6:00 PM)
1. Review open positions
2. Adjust stops to breakeven if applicable
3. Update trading journal
4. Set alerts for tomorrow's setups

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Trading without strength divergence confirmation
2. ❌ Adding multiple indicators (RSI, MACD, Stochastic)
3. ❌ Trading 4H signals against daily trend
4. ❌ Risking more than 2% per trade
5. ❌ Moving stops closer to entry
6. ❌ Revenge trading after losses
7. ❌ Overtrading when no clear setups exist
8. ❌ Ignoring major news events
