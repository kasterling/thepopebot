# Sterling Intelligence Strategy
## GBP/JPY Trading Framework

### Overview
This strategy focuses on GBP/JPY (Sterling/Yen) trading with emphasis on H4 (4-hour) timeframe analysis, combining technical levels with fundamental sentiment.

### Key Analysis Components

#### 1. Market Data Requirements
- Current GBP/JPY exchange rate
- Recent price action (support/resistance levels)
- Moving averages (20 EMA, 100 SMA)
- News and fundamental drivers

#### 2. Sentiment Analysis Framework
Evaluate sentiment based on:
- **Bullish Factors**: Strong UK economic data, weak JPY fundamentals, BoJ dovish stance
- **Bearish Factors**: UK weakness, JPY strength, BoJ hawkish moves, risk-off sentiment
- **Neutral/Mixed**: Conflicting signals, consolidation patterns

#### 3. H4 Timeframe Outlook
For each briefing, provide:
- **Bias**: Bullish / Bearish / Neutral
- **Key Levels**: Immediate support and resistance
- **Catalysts**: What's driving the current move
- **Risk Factors**: Potential reversal triggers
- **Target Zones**: Upside and downside targets

#### 4. Technical Framework
- **Trend**: Identify primary trend direction
- **Structure**: Support/resistance, trendlines, moving averages
- **Momentum**: RSI, divergences, momentum signals
- **Pattern**: Chart patterns, breakouts, reversals

### Briefing Format
Each Sterling Intelligence Briefing should include:
1. **Current Rate & Action**: Latest GBP/JPY level and recent move
2. **Fundamental Summary**: Key news and drivers (2-3 bullet points)
3. **Sentiment Assessment**: Overall market sentiment (bullish/bearish/mixed)
4. **H4 Outlook**: 
   - Bias direction
   - Key support/resistance levels
   - Short-term targets
   - Risk considerations
5. **Bottom Line**: One-sentence actionable summary

### Risk Considerations
- Japanese intervention risk above 215.00
- UK economic surprise potential
- BoJ policy shift risks
- Global risk sentiment shifts
- Correlations with broader FX markets
